Ang Li (李昂)

Welcome to my personal academic website!

I am an Assistant Professor at the Faculty of Law, University of Macau. My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and law, with a focus on legal large language models, information retrieval, and trustworthy AI for judicial applications.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Zhejiang University in 2026 and my bachelor's degree from Hunan University in 2021. During 2025–2026, I was a joint Ph.D. researcher at the Institute of Data Science, National University of Singapore, supervised by Prof. See-Kiong Ng.

Research Interests

  • Legal Judgment Prediction and Intelligent Judicial Decision Support
  • Legal Case Retrieval and Retrieval-Augmented Generation
  • Court View Generation and Legal Document Generation
  • Trustworthy and Explainable AI for Judicial Applications
  • Legal Simulation with Large Language Model Agents

Learn more about my background, research projects, and publications.
